Topic 3: Technical Infrastructure & Support Flow

The technical transition for the Global BCP Programme marks a shift from a "one-size-fits-all" support model to a tiered, entity-specific architecture. This evolution was designed to eliminate the inefficiencies of generalist support by deploying specialized resources where they are most effective.

3.1. Technical Achievements to Date
  • Service Edge Framework Establishment: The Service Edge (SE) framework was successfully initiated on January 23rd, 2026, establishing the foundational "L1 Direct clients FLM", "L2 Resellers / VIP", and "L3 Wholesale / Enterprise" tiers for all incoming traffic.

  • Support Standardization: Standardized processes for the "Hypercare" phase have been defined and documented in the Service Edge Handbook (V2), ensuring consistent response protocols during the high-alert window.

3.2. Current Focus: Intercompany SLA's

During the 30-day post-launch session held on 2 April, and as a critical component of the Global BCP programme, the focus now needs to shift towards finalizing intercompany SLAs.

The first draft of these Intercompany SLAs is expected by 29 May 2026 for the following entities:

  • ICTGlobe
  • Global MSP
  • Global BCP
  • DataPhone

It must be understood that the processes of identifying and defining unique inter-entity requirements, as well as formal agreements, may evolve as expectations and limitations become clearer; however, it is now crucial that the process of establishing at least the foundational requirements for each final Service Level Agreement between entities has started.

Points of Difficulty Currently Experienced:

  • 1. Labor Tracking Verifications: Verifying the precise, quantifiable measures required to accurately account for hours, labor, and technical call-outs included in the SLA scope, as well as tracking exactly when those allocations are depleted and must trigger additional billing.
  • 2. Cost Quantification: Verifying the actual underlying operational costs incurred by each independent entity to accurately structure and quantify backend on-billing to customers.
  • 3. Performance Metrics Evaluation: Determining the exact parameters for ongoing performance evaluation and establishing mutually agreed-upon methods or measurement mechanisms for dedicated outsourced staff.
  • 4. HR & Job Description Alignement: Identifying precise operational requirements that may directly affect an individual's existing Job Description (JD). These changes must be explicitly communicated to Human Resources to guarantee strict legal compliance with employment law before execution.

3.3. The Unifying Technical Backbone (Service Edge)

With the new Service Edge Handbook, it is anticipated to move toward a high-performance future where the technical backbone supports rapid, sustainable growth. Intercompany SLAs will be a vital component to ensure fair and sustainable service delivery across the entire organization.
