OPEN DISCUSSION

This session is aimed to address high-risk operational blockers, and define the exit criteria for the Hypercare (High-Alert) phase.

Thinking point:

Three Questions reflecting on your team's adaption

  • 1. Behavioral Gap: Where is your team still leaning on "old habits", and what one specific change is needed to help them fully own their new specialist role?
  • 2. Critical Setback: What was the single biggest friction point or concern for your devision this month, and how is it currently affecting your team's output?
  • 3. Stabilization Plan: What is your immediate strategy to fix "handover" gaps with other entities to ensure a move from the high-alert Hypercare phase to a stable, business-as-usual state?
