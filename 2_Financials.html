Topic 2: Financial Operations & Billing Integrity

Based on the previous 30-Day Post-Launch session, the state of Financial Operations underwent a period of high-intensity and manual interventions. These were implemented to protect the business while automated systems were under development to adjust to the new organizational structure.

2.1. Current Reality: The "Amber" Foundation

The current financial status remians AMBER. Potential risks exist but can be managed effectively with targeted intervention.

Finance & Billing Progress:
This was a crucial part of the project’s success. Although the team's great efforts during the first 30 days truly stood out, during our discussion held on 15 May, certain key factors were highlighted that should not be overlooked.

  • Billing Accuracy
    30 Day Review: For the March period, it was confirmed that all missing billing, including voice services, was fully integrated into the system.
    60 Day Review: For the April billing run, the confidence in billing accuracy shifted as it was pointed out that numerous clients were not invoiced. Contractual customers with missing end dates were not invoiced. It should duly be noted that individual cases of non-billing are being noticed as the month progresses. The team is actively conducting recons to establish the financial impact and locate any additional cases of billing errors to identify the root cause, which may involve multiple factors.
    Contractual customers with missing end dates, were not invoiced. It should dualy be noted that individual cases of non-billing are noticed as the month progresses.
    The team is actively conducting recon's to establish the impact and any additonal cases of billing errors, to identify the root of the couse which may conclude multiple factors to take into account.

  • Reconciliation
    30 Day Review: The March reconciliation confirmed that the figures matched the expected targets.
    60 Day Review: The April reconciliation shows a major risk. Apart from the clients who were not billed, vendor invoices have been received that cannot yet be linked to customers for on-billing, which may have a definite impact on current figures and expected targets.

  • Financial Volume Migration
    30 Day Review: At the end of March 2026, it was confirmed that the team successfully transitioned most of the revenue volume scheduled to move between entities, with any minor variances fully accounted for in non-recurring costs (NRC).
    60 Day Review: Taking into account the mentioned billing errors, it is recommended that the accuracy of the financial volume after migration be thoroughly evaluated once all billing errors have been identified and corrected.

  • Data Matching
    30 Day Review: For the March billing run, the team put in great effort to ensure that system data was cross-referenced against the master import spreadsheets. These efforts indicated that every client was billed correctly according to their specific contract terms.
    60 Day Review: Following corrective migrations between ICTGlobe and Global MSP during April, customer bases as divided between the entities proved to be inaccurate. A major cause identified for this issue relates to faulty data specifications (e.g., clients incorrectly marked as 'direct' vs. 'reseller'), mostly due to legacy billing requirements or special arrangements. It should be noted that while most of these cases have been identified and corrected, historical data or unaccounted-for instances may still exist.

  • Netcash Integration
    30 Day Review: The critical technical dependency during March existed because the Numista platform could not support dual Netcash instances. This prevented the clean, automated separation of payment gateways for the different entities.
    60 Day Review: Although the secondary Netcash account was approved as planned, a three-month window will be allowed to monitor Debit Order (DO) returns.
    The Netcash discussion opened a new window of focus, outlining a S.W.O.T. analysis which could be positively adopted within the programme:
    • Strengths
      Isolated Billing Infrastructure: Running separate Netcash instances provides clean financial lines, ensuring long-term data and billing integrity between entities.
      Operational Clarity: Eliminates cross-entity backend billing confusion from day one.
    • Weaknesses
      Brand Familiarity Gap: Customers navigating the transition are unfamiliar with the newly separated entity names.
      Mandate Friction: Reliance on customers to manually re-approve or sign new debit order mandates introduces an operational bottleneck.
    • Opportunities
      Two-Pronged Engagement Strategy: Mitigate uncertainty by deploying structured re-introductory / notice emails combined with direct customer engangement.
      Proactive Feedback Loops: Actively encouraging clients to voice concerns allows the business to address friction points early, turning a technical transition into a positive relationship-building exercise.
    • Threats
      Low Email Response Rates: Slow client adoption or ignored emails will delay mandate sign-offs.
      Collection Team Capacity: Delayed mandates will inevitably trigger a wave of manual, telephonic interventions, putting severe strain on collections teams already working under high pressure within tight timelines.
2.2. The Gap: Vision vs. Execution

The "Gap" represents the distance between Strategic Objectives and the Day-to-Day Reality

  • Objective (Scale & Resilience): Part of the goal is to create an automated billing engine that scales investment capacity.

  • Reality (Manual Burden): Current capacity is being heavily consumed by manual workarounds. The "Manual Update" burden identified is the primary gap; the system is not yet "resilient" enough to run without expert human oversight.
    • Purchase Orders (POs) cannot yet be automatically generated.
    • Connectivity items need to be manually linked to annuities on Numista.
    • New items added to annuities do not automatically bill the correct item price.
    • For new annuities, the pro-rata amount is not automatically generated.
    • When "Advance" is selected within annuities, the system erroneously shows arrears for all new services.
    • Fundamental billing information seems to be missing on Numista.
    • Although annuity billing of clients is correct on Numista, the corresponding invoices are processed under supplier accounts on Xero.
    • Porta billing results in errors in cases where there is a conflict with Bulling Platform IDs (BP IDs).
    • Item codes are active on Numista, yet fail on Xero due to invoice processing failures originating on Numista.

      • * Note: Developers are in the process of re-whitelabeling the system, which is scheduled to be completed by 22 June. Because ICTGlobe and Global MSP effectively run on the same underlying system, any changes will have a direct impact on both entities; therefore, all critical changes will need to be approved by ICTGlobe and handled with extreme caution.

Cash Flow Risk

The gap in customer mandate responses (Debit Orders) creates a temporary risk to predictable cash flow which may, temporarily, force the operational focus to shift from "Growth" to aggressive "Collections."
