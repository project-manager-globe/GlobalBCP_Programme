Topic 1: Strategic Vision vs. Current Reality

The foundational split of entities is complete, and the project has moved past the "High-Alert" Hypercare window. The current focus centers on optimization while actively managing any unforeseen hurdles.

60-Day Project Optimization Objectives

  • Strategy of Each Business Unit: Conduct a strategic breakaway to review, align, and thoroughly understand the strategic vision of each independent business.
    [Status: Complete]

  • SLA Review: Review and identify entity-unique business operations and requirements to ensure intercompany service level agreements (SLAs) are aligned accurately and fairly.
    [Status: In Progress]

  • Commercial Structure and Process Evaluation: During the 30-day follow-up discussion, it was recommended that each entity proactively engage with its clients. The goal is to establish whether the structural changes achieved an "Improved Customer Experience."
    • Note: This topic raised concerns regarding customer unawareness of the transition in some cases. While this must be tracked as a critical factor, client communication should simultaneously be approached as an opportunity.
    • Engagement Strategy: Customer friction or cancellations cannot be entirely avoided and must be acknowledged. However, with a clear and focused customer engagement strategy, new service and cross-selling opportunities can be unlocked.
    [Status: Ongoing]

Current Reality of the Global BCP Programme

The following section measures strategic objectives against current project reality, distinguishing between what has already been realized and what remains a future target.

  • Achieving Better Synergies & Focused Entities
    • Current Reality: The physical and operational separation into ICTGlobe, Global MSP, and Service Edge is fully in place.
    • 30-Day Gap: Staff members were still actively adjusting to the operational transition from generalist teams to specialized, strategically distinct entities.
      60-Day Review: Personnel are successfully adapting to the new organizational layout, independent workflows, and defined roles.
      Recommendation: Close evaluation by line managers remains highly recommended to continue smoothing staff adaptation through the final phases of the transition.

  • Market Share, Competitive Capability & Growth
    • Current Reality: This is classified as a Long-Term Objective.
    • Status: Activities targeting an increase in market share are scheduled for the formal Growth & Scale Phase (60–100 days post-go-live).

  • Higher Company Values & Cross-Selling
    • Current Reality: This is classified as a Future Objective.
    • Status: The initial Project Charter identifies cross-selling as a primary value driver; however, current engineering and operational efforts remain heavily focused on Billing Integrity. Before cross-selling can effectively occur, the "manual update" burden on billing must be fully resolved to guarantee a professional, accurate customer experience (as outlined in Topic 2).

  • Growth & Diversification (Tech, Products, Services)
    • Current Reality: The Systems (Numista, Xero, Aluxium) have been integrated and are now partialy opperational, improvements and sytem changes are still ongoing.

  • Attracting & Retaining Quality Human Capital
    • Current Reality: This remains a sensitive operational Hurdle.
    • Status: The restructuring has underwent a period of staff adjustments. Success here depends on "Talent Review", scheduled for the 61–100 day window. "Quality" must be maintained through high-intensity training on the new Service Edge handbook and role-clarity workshops to reduce any anxiety that may be caused due to the entity split.The restructuring has undergone an expected period of staff adjustments. Strategic Takeaway

To reach the long-term goals of Growth and Market Share, the immediate reality requires a hard shift from Manual Intervention to System Autonomy. The BCP project can only unlock its full commercial value once daily manual workarounds shift completely to automated system resources.
